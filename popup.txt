Header:
Traveling With 19+ Passengers?

Text:
Your passenger count may be better suited to a Group Charter aircraft.

Group Charter provides access to larger aircraft specifically configured for groups of 19 or more passengers and may provide more suitable aircraft availability and pricing for your trip.

You can switch to Group Charter or continue searching private jet options.

Primary button:
View Group Charter Options

Secondary button:
Continue With Private Jet

Workflow:
Primary → change trip_classification to Group Charter and continue search.
Secondary → dismiss popup and continue with Airplane.

Header:
A Private Jet May Be a Better Fit

Text:
VIP Airliners are typically selected for larger groups or missions requiring exceptionally large cabins and specialized configurations.

For a smaller group, a Heavy or Ultra Long Range private jet may provide significantly more aircraft options while still offering a large cabin and long-range capability.

You can switch to private jet options or continue with your VIP Airliner request.

Primary button:
View Private Jet Options

Secondary button:
Continue With VIP Airliner

Workflow:
Primary → change trip_classification to Airplane.
Secondary → dismiss and continue.

I’d use under 10 PAX, not 19, because there are legitimate BBJ/ACJ requests with relatively few passengers.

Trigger:

Header:
Is This a Cargo Flight?

Text:
Cargo aircraft are primarily used for transporting freight rather than passengers.

If your request is for passenger travel, select Private Jet or Group Charter instead. If you are transporting cargo and the passenger count represents accompanying personnel, you can continue with your request.

Primary button:
Continue With Cargo

Secondary button:
View Passenger Aircraft

Workflow:
Primary → dismiss and continue.
Secondary → change to Airplane or return them to the aircraft-type selector.

I would not automatically block Cargo with PAX > 0, because some cargo missions can include authorized accompanying personnel.
